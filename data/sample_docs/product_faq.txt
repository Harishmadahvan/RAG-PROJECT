# Acme Corp — Product FAQ (Sample)

## Product Overview
Acme Retail Cloud helps stores manage inventory, orders, and customer loyalty in one dashboard.
The product supports multi-store setups and role-based access for managers and staff.

## Pricing Plans
- Starter: $49/month — up to 1 store and 3 users.
- Growth: $149/month — up to 5 stores and 20 users.
- Enterprise: custom pricing — unlimited stores, SSO, and dedicated support.

## Free Trial
New customers get a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card is required for the trial.
Trial data is deleted 30 days after the trial ends unless the customer upgrades.

## Integrations
Acme Retail Cloud integrates with Shopify, WooCommerce, and major payment gateways.
API access is available on Growth and Enterprise plans.

## Support SLA
- Starter: email support within 2 business days.
- Growth: email + chat support within 1 business day.
- Enterprise: 24x7 support with 4-hour response for critical issues.
