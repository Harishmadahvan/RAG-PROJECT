# Acme Corp — Employee Handbook (Sample)

## About Acme Corp
Acme Corp is a mid-size technology company that builds cloud software for retail customers.
Work hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, with flexible start times between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM.

## Leave Policy
- Permanent employees get 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Probation employees (first 6 months) get 1 day of leave per month.
- Sick leave: 8 days per year for all employees.
- Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 2 working days in advance for planned leave.
- Emergency leave can be applied on the same day with manager approval.

## Work From Home (WFH)
- Employees may take up to 2 WFH days per week.
- WFH must be approved by the reporting manager in the HR portal.
- During probation, WFH is limited to 1 day per week.

## Salary and Payroll
- Salary is credited on the last working day of each month.
- Payslips are available in the employee self-service portal.
- Tax declarations must be submitted before 15th January each year.

## IT Support
- For laptop or access issues, raise a ticket at support.acme.example.com.
- Critical production issues should be escalated to the on-call engineer via Slack channel #prod-alerts.
